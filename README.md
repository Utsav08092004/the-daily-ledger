# The Daily Ledger (India Edition) 🇮🇳
> **Indian Financial & Economic Intelligence in Plain English**

A modern, automated daily digital newspaper designed to empower everyday Indian financial decisions with zero confusing jargon.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

1. **🔴 Real-Time Dalal Street Benchmarks**:
   - Streaming quotes for **NIFTY 50, BSE SENSEX, NIFTY BANK, NIFTY IT, USD / INR (₹), MCX Gold (₹/10g), MCX Silver (₹/kg), India 10Y Yield, Brent Crude, and Bitcoin (INR)**.
   - High-frequency 8-second auto-stream with tick counters and intraday high/low day range sliders.

2. **🏢 India Inc. Corporate Quarterly Results Desk (`/earnings`)**:
   - Detailed quarterly scorecards in **₹ Crores** for India's blue-chip giants (**Reliance Industries, TCS, HDFC Bank, Tata Motors, Zomato / Blinkit, ITC Limited**).
   - Revenue beats/misses, Net Profit (PAT), plain-English verdicts, and portfolio impact for mutual fund / retail investors.

3. **📰 Multi-Source Automated Live News Wire**:
   - Continuous 30-second automated ingestion from **The Economic Times, Livemint, and Financial Express**.
   - Auto-translated into 60-second summaries, **💡 ELI5 Everyday Analogies**, and **👛 Rupee Wallet Action Rules**.

4. **🧮 Indian Wealth Decision Laboratory (`/tools`)**:
   - **🏠 Home Loan & RBI Repo Rate Hike Simulator**: Test 0.25%/0.50% rate changes on monthly EMIs and calculate the power of 1 extra annual prepayment.
   - **🌱 Mutual Fund SIP & 12.5% LTCG Tax Compounding**: Calculate future wealth with a 10% annual step-up and post-tax take-home wealth after the ₹1.25L annual exemption.
   - **🔥 Inflation vs FD vs PPF vs Nifty 50 Matrix**: Compare real wealth preservation over 5-25 years.

5. **🧠 Indian Financial Jargon Buster (`/glossary`)**:
   - Plain-English explanations and analogies for **RBI Repo Rate, 12.5% LTCG, SIPs, Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGB), Demat & CDSL/NSDL, PPF & 80C, and Indexation**.

6. **✉️ Daily Morning Email Dispatch Engine (`/admin`)**:
   - Automated morning newspaper HTML generation and subscriber broadcast center.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Framework**: Next.js 14 (App Router) + TypeScript
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS + Custom Newspaper Ivory / Vintage Sepia / Midnight Dark themes
- **Icons**: Lucide React
- **Data Feeds**: Yahoo Finance API (NSE/BSE real-time tickers) + Live RSS News Feeds (ET, Livemint)

## 📦 Deployment Guide

### Deploying to Vercel (100% Free - Recommended)

1. Upload this folder to your GitHub repository (e.g. `github.com/your-username/the-daily-ledger`).
2. Go to **[vercel.com](https://vercel.com)** and log in with GitHub.
3. Click **"Add New Project"** and select `the-daily-ledger`.
4. Click **"Deploy"** (Vercel automatically detects Next.js).
5. Within 1 minute, your site will be publicly live at `https://your-project.vercel.app`!
